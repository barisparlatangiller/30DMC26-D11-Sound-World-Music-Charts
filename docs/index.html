About this map

Charts. Spotify weekly Top 200 for 37 countries + global (Türkiye and global weekly, the others every 4 weeks) and YouTube weekly Top 100 for 43 countries + global, from the open archive on Zenodo (record 23645386, CC BY 4.0). Period: 1 January – 15 October 2026, the last week in the archive.

Points. A chart position is worth (N + 1 − rank) / N, shared by the credited artists, and averaged over the charted weeks so weekly and 4-weekly charts compare. An artist's share is its part of all points in that country. "Both" averages the Spotify and YouTube shares.

Genres. From Wikidata (genre, P136), matched by the artists' Spotify and Google IDs, grouped into 12 families by keyword. Artists without a Wikidata genre are "unclassified". Home-grown: the artist's home country from the archive (Wikidata or chart origin); the US includes Puerto Rico.

Spotify vs YouTube: overlap of the two artist shares (1 = identical). Musical twins: cosine similarity of two countries' artist shares.

Limits. Charts show the most-streamed songs, not all listening; Spotify streams and YouTube views are different scales, so only ranks are used. Countries without charts in the archive are grey.
